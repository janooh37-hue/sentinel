The same in all three

  1. One language per form. An Arabic session asks in Arabic, an English session asks in English. No «· AR» / «· EN» pairs, and nothing is required in the other language. Applies to accident reports, maintenance, add vehicle and sites.
  2. The Arabic letter asks once. If a report was written in English, the official-letter step asks for the Arabic wording before the letter is saved. Open accident 16 \ 82541 and press «Official letter» to see it.
  3. No damage cost to type. The report states that insurance covers the damage and that the technician estimates the cost.
  4. Insurance details are optional. Claim number, claim status, insurance company, repair garage and who bears the cost can be added when filing or at any time later, and none of them blocks anything.
  5. A report can be closed without a cost.
  6. Numbers and money. Latin digits in both languages, whole dirhams, and the dirham mark the fines pages already use.

All vehicles, people, plates, claims and amounts in the mockups are fabricated. Labels come from the product's own Arabic and English copy. Steps this work does not change (renew licence, edit vehicle, fleet import, add fine) show a short notice instead of opening.
