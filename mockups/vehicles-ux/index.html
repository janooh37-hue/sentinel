Rules carried over

  1. One language per form. An Arabic session asks in Arabic, an English session asks in English, and nothing is required in the other language.
  2. The Arabic letter asks once. If a report was written in English, the official-letter step asks for the Arabic wording. Open accident 16 \ 82541 and press «Official letter» to see it.
  3. No damage cost to type. The report states that insurance covers the damage and that the technician estimates the cost.
  4. Insurance details are optional and can be added at filing or later. A report can be closed without them.
  5. Numbers and money. Latin digits in both languages, whole dirhams, and the dirham mark.

All vehicles, people, plates, claims and amounts are fabricated. Labels come from the product's own Arabic and English copy. Steps this work does not change (renew licence, edit vehicle, fleet import, add fine, request maintenance) show a short notice instead of opening.
