Mobile bottom nav — redesign variants

The bug: the app ships without viewport-fit=cover, so env(safe-area-inset-bottom) is 0 on iPhone. The bar sits flush on the physical screen edge: the display's rounded corners slice its far left/right, and the home indicator lands on the tabs. Browser + screenshots hide it because Safari's own chrome pads the bottom. Every variant below fixes it — two geometries: full-bleed (bar extends under the home indicator, corners covered by its own background) or floating (bar inset from the edges, physically can't touch the corners).
