GSSG Manager — upload control + send confirmation · review mockup v2

v2 per your feedback: no hard-disable. Send always opens a confirmation popup — “Ready to send?” with the message previewed in a phone frame, Send under it, or Continue editing. If “Upload a file” (or “Attach a record”) was chosen with nothing attached, the same popup carries a warning and the button becomes “Send anyway”. Section 1 is the upload control redesign; section 2 is the live end-to-end demo — type, toggle the attachment, press Send.
