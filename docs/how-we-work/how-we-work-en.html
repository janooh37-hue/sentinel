01

Starting a Request

Every request starts in one place: the Services screen. The officer picks the form, selects the employee, and the employee's details fill in from their file. There is nothing to copy from old documents and nothing to retype.

The Services screen — every form starts here.

The form, filled — the employee's details arrive on their own.

02

Creating the Document

The moment the form is saved, the official document is created. It receives its reference number automatically — issued in sequence, never repeated — with a scannable code stamped on the page. The finished PDF is ready the same second. For annual leave, the undertaking form is prepared with it, under the same reference.

The document, seconds after saving — numbered, stamped, and ready.

03

Approval and Signature

The request goes straight to the manager's approval queue. The manager reviews the actual document — at the desk or on the phone — and approves with one tap. The signature is placed into the official document, and the record locks so nothing changes after signing.

Previously: printed, carried from desk to desk, and waiting until the right person was in the office.

The approval queue — everything waiting, in one list.

Approval from the phone — wherever the manager is.

04

Notifying the Employee

As soon as the document is issued, the employee receives a text message in their own language — Arabic or English — with the dates and the details. No one has to remember to inform them, and every message is kept on the employee's file.

Previously: the employee asked at the office, or waited to hear.

The employee's messages — sent automatically, kept on file.

05

Related Correspondence

Letters and emails are written on the same screen where the records live. Typing an employee number or a reference number turns it into a direct link, and the referenced document attaches itself to the email. What is sent is exactly what is on file.

An email in progress — the reference becomes a link, the document attaches itself.

06

Incoming Scans

When a signed paper comes back — from the scanner or by email — it lands in one inbox. The system reads it, recognizes the reference, and files it to the right record on its own. The officer only confirms.

Previously: scan, rename, sort into folders, then write and send an email.

The scan inbox — papers file themselves; the officer confirms.

07

The Completed Record

The signed document rests in the employee's file, next to everything else that concerns them. The leave balance updates by itself, the yearly report reflects it immediately, and anyone who needs the record finds it in seconds.

The employee's file — the complete record, in one place.

Oversight

Management sees the whole picture on one screen: who is on leave today, what is waiting for approval, and which passports and visas expire soon. No one has to ask for a status report — the status is always in view.

The management dashboard — the whole picture, at a glance.

Looking Ahead — Notes for the Next Level

The path above is working today. These are my notes on where it can go next.

WhatsApp alongside SMS

The WhatsApp channel is already built into the system and waits for activation. Once approved, notifications can reach employees on the channel they check most.

Automatic reminders

The system knows when a leave ends and when a passport or visa expires. The next step is letting it remind people on its own — a message before the return date, an alert before the expiry.

Employee self-service

Today, requests start at the HR desk. The natural next step is letting employees open their own requests from their phones, with the same path handling approval and filing.

Smarter document reading

Work on reading passports and IDs automatically has already started. Completing it — and widening it to more document types — will remove the last of the manual typing.

A monthly management summary

The data is already in one place. A one-page monthly summary — leaves, approvals, violations — can be produced automatically for management.
